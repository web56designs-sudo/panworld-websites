Maritime Operational Workflow

  1. 01.

    Carrier Space & Port Routing

    Select optimal discharge port (Apapa, Tin Can, Lekki Deep Sea, or Onne) based on draft and consignee proximity.

  2. 02.

    Bill of Lading & Form M Alignment

    Verify consignee, HS codes, and container seal numbers prior to vessel sailing to eliminate manifest amendments.

  3. 03.

    Discharge & Customs Assessment

    Coordinate terminal delivery orders (TDO), PAAR processing, and shipping line release upon vessel arrival.

  4. 04.

    Gate-Out & Empty Container Return

    Deploy dedicated flatbed haulage and manage timely empty container return to prevent detention charges.
