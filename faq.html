01 / Regulatory Brokerage

Customs Clearance & Documentation

Clean pre-arrival documentation prevents customs queries, valuation disputes, and terminal delays. Panworld Nigeria Limited reviews the following core file set before vessel or aircraft arrival:

  • Original or Telex Release Bill of Lading (B/L) / Master Air Waybill (MAWB) matching consignee and cargo weight.
  • Final Commercial Invoice & Combined Certificate of Value and Origin (CCVO) aligned with ECOWAS CET HS tariff codes.
  • Detailed Packing List specifying gross/net weights, package counts, and container or pallet breakdowns.
  • CBN Approved Form M registered on the Trade Monitoring System (TRMS) with valid local marine insurance.
  • Pre-Arrival Assessment Report (PAAR) issued via Nigeria Customs Service (NCS) for NICIS II SGD filing.
  • Agency Permits (where applicable): SON Product Certificate & SONCAP activation, NAFDAC import clearance for regulated food/pharma goods, or NESREA approvals.

Need a document pre-audit? Submit your cargo pack details via our Customs Clearance Desk.

When Form M, PAAR, and regulatory permits are validated prior to arrival, Panworld typically achieves:

  • Air Cargo (LOS & ABV): 24–48 hours from cargo shed check-in at NAHCO or SAHCO through NCS duty release and van dispatch.
  • Sea Freight Containers (Apapa, Tin Can, Lekki & Onne): 3–6 working days following vessel berth, container discharge, joint physical/scanner examination, and Terminal Delivery Order (TDO) issuance.

Timelines depend on prompt duty settlement, shipping-line release turnaround, and whether multi-agency sampling (NAFDAC/SON) is triggered on regulated commodities.

03 / Pre-Arrival & Working Capital Protection

Import Finance & Demurrage Prevention

Port demurrage and terminal storage charges escalate sharply once shipping-line free days expire. Panworld supports commercial importers and multinational manufacturers in two decisive ways:

  • Rapid Clearance Disbursement & Financial Structuring: For established corporate accounts, PNL provides structured clearance disbursement support and rapid settlement coordination on terminal handling, shipping-line charges, and ancillary port fees so release files do not stall waiting on piecemeal approvals.
  • Upfront Landed-Cost & Form M Alignment: Our Import Finance Advisory Desk forecasts exact import duties, CET levies, VAT, CISS, and terminal charges prior to supplier PO release while auditing Letters of Credit and Form M packs so Authorized Dealer Banks clear FX/duty payments without discrepancy holds.

Most demurrage originates before the ship docks. Panworld eliminates avoidable dwell time by negotiating extended ocean free-time terms where possible, running pre-arrival PAAR and manifest audits prior to vessel ETA, transferring uncleared containers to bonded holding hubs when duty phasing is needed, and pre-booking haulage trucks before final customs exit.

04 / Multi-Modal Execution

Freight, Warehousing & Tracking

  • FCL (Full Container Load): Exclusive use of 20ft, 40ft, High Cube, or Reefer containers. Ideal for bulk raw materials, commercial restocks, and machinery exceeding 15 CBM where sealed unit integrity and lower per-unit ocean freight matter.
  • LCL (Less-than-Container Load): Shared container consolidation for smaller palletized consignments (typically 1–12 CBM), billed strictly on cargo volume/weight and deconsolidated at our Lagos bonded terminals.
  • Air Freight: Fastest door-to-door transit (2–7 days depending on Express Priority vs. Consolidated tier) for urgent production spares, medical/pharma cold-chain, electronics, and high-value cargo arriving via LOS, ABV, PHC, or KAN.

Our Warehousing & Distribution facilities across the Apapa corridor, Ikeja/Ogba industrial zone, Port Harcourt/Onne, and Abuja provide short-, medium-, and long-term storage. Options include customs-bonded bays for duty-deferred cargo, high-bay ambient pallet racking with FIFO/FEFO batch control, 24-hour container destuffing and cross-docking, and reinforced hardstand open yards for steel pipes, coils, and oversized machinery.
