Air Freight Execution Sequence

  1. 01.

    Routing & Chargeable Weight Audit

    We verify gross vs. volumetric weight (1:6000 ratio) and confirm optimal airline routing before pickup.

  2. 02.

    Pre-Arrival PAAR & MAWB Alignment

    Draft Air Waybill and commercial documents are cross-checked against Form M and HS codes prior to uplift.

  3. 03.

    Shed Examination & Duty Release

    Dedicated airport brokers manage NCS physical inspection and terminal charges within 24–48 hours.

  4. 04.

    Direct Plant or Warehouse Handover

    Sealed delivery straight to your consignee facility with signed electronic Proof of Delivery.
