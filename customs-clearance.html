Customs Release Sequence

  1. 01.

    Pre-Arrival Manifest & PAAR Audit

    Verify Bill of Lading / MAWB manifest alignment, PAAR duty valuation, and regulatory permits before berth.

  2. 02.

    SGD Assessment & Duty Settlement

    Transmit assessment notices directly for electronic bank duty payment and receipt confirmation.

  3. 03.

    Joint Physical / Scanner Examination

    Panworld terminal officers represent your shipment during NCS, SON, NAFDAC, and NDLEA inspections.

  4. 04.

    Exit Note & Terminal Delivery Order

    Secure Customs Exit Note and terminal TDO for immediate dispatch onto Panworld haulage trucks.
