Company Overview

Indigenous Logistics & Licensed Customs Expertise Since 1999

Established in 1999 with headquarters in Lagos, Panworld Nigeria Limited (PNL) specializes in import clearing, international freight forwarding, interstate haulage, warehousing, procurement, and trade consultancy. With over a decade of hands-on experience serving multinational corporations, PNL pairs a flexible, customer-responsive approach with ongoing operational innovations and documented SOP checkpoints. All official instructions and rate confirmations are maintained in writing by our operations desk.

01. Single-Point Accountability

Instead of fragmenting responsibility between overseas forwarders, local customs agents, procurement desks, and haulage fleets, PNL coordinates the entire supply chain under one shipment file.

02. Flexible, Multinational-Tested Service

Backed by over a decade of supporting multinational standards, our operations adapt flexibly to urgent manufacturing schedules, project cargo timelines, and specialized regulatory requirements.

03. Ongoing Operational Innovation

Continuous refinements across pre-arrival compliance audits, digital documentation tracking, and landed-cost modeling keep port dwell times predictable and eliminate unbudgeted terminal charges.

04. Regulatory & Agency Alignment

Active licensed brokerage coverage across Nigeria Customs Service (NCS), NPA, NIMASA, FAAN, SON, and NAFDAC inspection desks at Nigeria's primary seaports and air terminals.
