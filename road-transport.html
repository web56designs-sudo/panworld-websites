Haulage Dispatch Workflow

  1. 01.

    Terminal Call-Up & Truck Staging

    Trucks are pre-booked on terminal call-up systems immediately upon customs release validation.

  2. 02.

    Seal & Equipment Inspection

    Pre-departure verification of container numbers, customs seal integrity, and waybill documentation.

  3. 03.

    Corridor Transit & Milestone Alerts

    Written checkpoint updates sent via email along Southwest, East, and Abuja/Northern highway corridors.

  4. 04.

    Offloading & Empty Return Receipt

    Consignee offloading confirmation followed by shipping-line empty container drop-off and EIR filing.
